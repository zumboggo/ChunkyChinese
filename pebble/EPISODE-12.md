# Chunky Chinese — Episode 12, The Nice One

For Pebble Time 2 / Emery. App version 1.6.0.

1. Open **chunky-chinese-time2-supportive-12-v1.6.0.pbw** on your Android phone with the Pebble app. Install it over the existing Chunky Chinese app; do not uninstall first.
2. If an older downloaded episode opens, use **Back → Library → Read included Ep 12**. Previous episode positions are retained.
3. In the Pebble phone app, open **Chunky Chinese → Settings**. Paste the same **Hearth Garden connection key** that your watchface uses, then save. Leave the episode URL blank to use Episode 12's normal feed.
4. Read the whole episode. Its ending earns one credit. Return to Hearth Garden while your phone has Bluetooth and internet; its normal sync fills the next red Chinese circle. Your existing watchface does not need reinstalling.

If you need the connection key again: Chunky Chinese website → Settings → Account & Sync → Hearth & Garden. A newly generated key for the same account also connects to the same garden.

After this one-time upgrade, Refresh also accepts new supported animation recipes. See [downloadable motion](scenes/downloadable.md) for the current character and movement limits. New character designs, fonts and renderer capabilities still require a PBW.

## Reading

Select advances, Up revisits, Down switches pinyin/English to Chinese/pinyin, and Back opens the menu. Choices require confirmation. Animations hold their final pose until a fresh Select press; Down replays them. Six animated scenes accompany the original four choices.

The whole episode works offline. Completed readings wait safely for connection; retries and revisiting the ending do not earn duplicate circles. Restarting and finishing a whole reread earns another circle. Old partially read saves are not retroactively counted.

Hearth Garden retains its existing Shanghai Sunday–Saturday week: seven red circles, then +N for extras, with weekly gem progress. Watch reading positions remain separate from phone positions; only completion credit joins the shared Garden totals.
