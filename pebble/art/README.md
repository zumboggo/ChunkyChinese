# Artwork provenance

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-11-13 for David's personal Mandarin reader. References: existing approved `stories/characters/super-supportive/supportive-11-opening.png` and `alden-approved-a.png`, using Episode 11's cast identities. These are stylized learning-app portraits, not official novel illustrations.

The generation brief requested static, restrained pixel art with recognizable consistent character designs, readable faces, a white portrait background, and no text. The 4 × 4 atlas is ordered left to right, top to bottom:

1. Hannah neutral, warm, worried, sad
2. Hannah determined, excited; young Alden curious, worried
3. Young Alden confused, thoughtful; present-day Alden thoughtful, sad
4. Present-day Alden determined, neutral; young Alden neutral; Hannah calm

Opening: Hannah's conversation with young Alden at the cafe. Closing: present-day Alden reflecting during his train journey. These are static scene illustrations; no animation or paid video was generated.

`prepare-art.mjs` crops the atlas and produces final 64 × 64 and checked 48 × 48 palette PNGs using nearest-neighbor scaling. The source images are larger generation masters; the watch assets are the authored delivery dimensions. All requested 13 expressions are included, with three extra neutral/calm variants for reuse.
